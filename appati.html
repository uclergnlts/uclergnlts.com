Appati · macOS menu bar app

Your apps' numbers, at the edge of your Mac.

Users, traffic, subscribers, revenue and reviews for every app you make, in one panel and in the MacBook notch. No account, no server.

Download for Mac Source on GitHub ↗

Free · open source (MIT) · signed and notarized · updates itself · macOS 12 or later

What it does

One panel for every app

⌥⌘A

Active users, sign-ups, visitors, subscribers, MRR, store reviews, search clicks and YouTube stats. Each app gets its own card; the top shows what needs your attention today.

A live summary in the notch

Notch

Move the pointer to the notch and today's numbers drop down, next to the song you're playing with Spotify and YouTube controls.

Funnels and a weekly summary

Insight

See where people drop between visiting, signing up and paying. Every week starts with a notification comparing the last seven days to the week before.

Your keys stay on your Mac

Privacy

Keys are encrypted with the macOS Keychain and requests go straight to the services you connect. Appati has no backend and collects nothing.

Works with what you already use

Users

Pick one

Supabase, Firebase, or a custom endpoint: one URL on your own backend that returns JSON.

Site traffic

Pick one

Umami (with its free share link), Plausible or Google Analytics 4.

Revenue

Pick one

RevenueCat for in-app subscriptions, Stripe for the web. A read-only key is enough.

Stores, search and video

Optional

App Store Connect, Google Play, Google Search Console and YouTube.

Good to know

Four languages

i18n

English, Türkçe, Español and العربية. It follows your Mac's language; you can change it in Settings.

Try it before connecting anything

Sample data

The panel opens with sample numbers, clearly labelled, so you can look around first. Every source has a “Test connection” button.
